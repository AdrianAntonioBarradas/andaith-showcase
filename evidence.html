Andaith / Behind the project

How this demo was made

Illustration, backed by execution.

The animation is a purpose-built explanation of Andaith’s workflow. It is not a recording of a shipping web interface or a real Telegram conversation. Message text is shortened or translated into English for readability.

The underlying behavior was checked with selected, unchanged application packages: real scheduling rules, SQLite persistence, and MCP calls in an isolated process. An injected clock, a temporary database, and a simulated delivery adapter keep the demonstration separate from the personal installation.

The illustrated plan, intention and result follow the fictional Atlas scenario. The missing-response state reflects the recorded absence of a check-in, not a conclusion about activity completion. External research is not run: the digest content is invented and supplied to the notification tool.

Checks behind the story

  • Valid plan import; an overlapping addition is rejected.
  • A notified block is protected from removal.
  • Start and close check-ins persist; reminders and a missing-response state are recorded.
  • A recorded notification survives database reopen without ordinary repeat delivery.
  • MCP reads the plan and sends a digest through the simulated adapter.
  • A quiet-hour message becomes an inbox note instead of being delivered.

The nine scenario assertions passed with Go’s race detector. The original repository’s test suite also passed during investigation. These checks do not establish exhaustive correctness, security, or availability.

Voice and animation

The creator recorded the English narration. Pauses were trimmed and volume adjusted; scenes follow the recordings rather than equal slide durations. Speech is not time-stretched. Captions and the readable transcript are edited from the narration script.

The video uses local HTML/CSS animation with a deterministic timeline. Narration, captions, and scene changes share that timeline. Audio is normalized and checked after AAC encoding.

Project status and limits

Andaith is a personal project with one user: its creator. The source and private execution harness are not distributed here. The contact invitation starts a conversation; it is not a public signup, available download, or commitment to support.

A persistent ledger avoids ordinary repeat notifications after restart, but delivery and recording are separate operations. Exactly-once delivery is not guaranteed. Contextual digests do not establish accuracy of news, traffic, weather, or vulnerability detection.

No analytics, interest counter, contact form, or visitor storage is added by this page. The email link opens the visitor’s mail application; the page does not send or store a message.
