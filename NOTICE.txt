Synthetic narration

The voice-over is synthesized with Kokoro-82M, using the built-in af_sarah voice through the local text2audio project (pykokoro 0.8.4, CUDA, fp32). It is not the project owner’s recorded or cloned voice. First-person statements read the owner’s account as described in the case study.

The Kokoro model card and the timestamped ONNX distribution identify their model license as Apache-2.0. Sarah is listed in the accompanying voice inventory. No separate restriction specific to af_sarah was found in the reviewed model and voice documentation. This package distributes the generated narration only, not model weights, voice embeddings, or the synthesis software.
